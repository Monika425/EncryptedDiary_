# External photography credits

The UI uses photographs hosted by Unsplash directly from `images.unsplash.com` rather than generated `.webp` mockup assets. These photos are provided under the Unsplash License; check the linked source pages for current terms.

- Kelly Sikkema, “A desk with a notepad, notebook, and flowers”: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-desk-with-a-notepad-notebook-and-flowers-AofgbZrFyGA
- Kelly Sikkema, “An open notebook sitting on top of a desk next to a flower”: https://unsplash.com/photos/an-open-notebook-sitting-on-top-of-a-desk-next-to-a-flower-9kMNh_ZOFHI
